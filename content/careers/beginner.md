## Foundations to build

Learn files, permissions, processes, IP addresses, DNS and HTTP. Explain a browser request from your laptop to a server in your own words.

Start with the [Security+ foundation lessons](/academy/) and keep a list of concepts you can explain without notes. You do not need to collect certifications before doing small, authorized projects.

## A practical progression

1. Reproduce a small guided task and record the inputs and expected outcome.
2. Change one assumption, predict the result, and test it.
3. Write down a failure you encountered and how you diagnosed it.
4. Ask a peer to repeat your instructions on a clean environment.

Use a disposable Linux VM to inspect a local listener, then analyze the included DNS capture. Keep a short glossary tied to observations. Begin with the [related guided lab](/labs/linux-hardening/) and explore [wireshark](/tools/wireshark/) when it supports a specific question.

## Portfolio evidence

Publish a local lab diagram and a reproducible troubleshooting note. Use synthetic data and show what failed before it worked. Include a README, scope, setup, original or properly licensed sample data, verification steps and limitations. Remove credentials, personal data and employer or university information that you are not authorized to share. A smaller project that another person can reproduce is more useful than a long tool list without evidence.

## Certification planning

Network+ or ISC2 CC may be research starting points; current eligibility and objectives must be checked with providers. Study resources here are free; certification examinations and some external training are not. Treat the [certification planning records](/academy/certifications/) as research prompts, not verified eligibility advice.

## Internships, applications and community

Choose legitimate entry-level roles whose tasks overlap your demonstrated work. Tailor your resume with accurate examples: the problem, your action, the evidence and the result. Do not claim production experience for a synthetic lab. Track applications and prepare a short explanation of one project’s design tradeoff. Club competitions can provide useful team practice when their rules and scope are clear. No path or credential guarantees employment.
