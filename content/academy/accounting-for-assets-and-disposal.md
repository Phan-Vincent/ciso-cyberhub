## Understand the concept

Inventory connects a technical object to an owner, purpose, sensitivity and lifecycle state. An unknown service cannot be reliably patched or retired. Record software and data assets as well as devices. Disposal must address retained data, copied credentials and external dependencies.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

A retired laptop may still have cloud tokens, browser sessions and data on removable media. Sanitizing its local drive alone does not revoke those credentials. Keep evidence of the disposal process and follow the organization’s retention requirements before destroying records.

## Practice and explain

Create an inventory for a three-system lab. Include owner, operating system, role, update responsibility, data sensitivity and planned retirement date.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

Why assign an asset owner?

**Expected reasoning:** Someone must be accountable for access, maintenance, risk decisions and retirement. Erasing storage does not invalidate a copied credential or an independently active remote session.
