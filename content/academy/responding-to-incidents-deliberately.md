## Understand the concept

Preparation establishes roles, communication paths, collection procedures and authority. During an incident, validate the signal, scope affected assets, contain harm, remove the cause, recover services and review lessons. These activities can overlap; evidence and business impact should guide the sequence.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

Immediately powering off a suspect system may stop harm but can destroy volatile evidence. Decide with the incident lead based on safety, ongoing loss and collection capability. Preserve notes explaining what happened and why. Never delay an urgent protective action just to satisfy a rigid checklist.

## Practice and explain

Use the incident-response lab to produce a short timeline, containment recommendation and recovery verification plan. Label every inference as such.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

Why conduct a post-incident review?

**Expected reasoning:** To improve controls and response based on evidence, rather than assign unsupported blame. Response decisions balance evidence preservation with immediate safety and damage reduction.
