## Understand the concept

Compliance connects obligations to controls and evidence. Requirements can arise from laws, contracts and internal policy; their applicability depends on context. Security outcomes and compliance evidence overlap, but neither can be inferred solely from the other. Route uncertain legal interpretations to an appropriate qualified owner.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

For a retention requirement, document what records are covered, when the clock begins, who authorizes deletion and how holds are handled. Collect evidence that the control operated during the relevant period. A screenshot from one day does not prove year-round enforcement.

## Practice and explain

Build an evidence matrix with requirement, control, owner, collection frequency and retention. Use a fictional internal policy rather than making a legal-compliance claim.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

Does meeting a checklist guarantee security?

**Expected reasoning:** No. Scope, implementation quality and threats outside the checklist still matter. Control design and control operation are different assurance questions.
