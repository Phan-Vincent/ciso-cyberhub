## Understand the concept

Third-party assessment covers more than a questionnaire. Define what data and access the supplier receives, review relevant evidence, set incident notification expectations and plan exit or replacement. Reassess material changes in ownership, service scope or access.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

A hosted project tool may be free but still hold sensitive student information. Evaluate export options, deletion behavior and administrator access before adoption. A supplier’s audit report has a scope and time period; it does not establish every service configuration is appropriate for your use.

## Practice and explain

Create a supplier checklist for a fictional hosted documentation service. Include data location questions, access review, export testing and an exit owner.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

Why test data export before committing to a service?

**Expected reasoning:** It validates an exit path and reveals dependencies before migration becomes urgent. Assurance evidence is meaningful only within its stated scope and period.
