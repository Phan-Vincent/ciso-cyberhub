## Understand the concept

An audit evaluates evidence against criteria. A vulnerability assessment identifies potential weaknesses. A penetration test uses authorized techniques to evaluate exploitability and impact within agreed boundaries. Establish scope, excluded assets, timing, communications and stop conditions before active testing.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

A public club website is not automatically permission to test university infrastructure. Ownership and authorization differ. A report should include reproducible evidence, impact, limitations, remediation guidance and retest results. Do not overstate a finding based only on an automated scanner label.

## Practice and explain

Write a rules-of-engagement outline for a local training application. Include what happens if an unexpected third-party dependency appears.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

What should accompany a reported technical finding?

**Expected reasoning:** Evidence, affected scope, impact, limitations, a fix and a verification method. A hyperlink or technical dependency does not grant permission to test another system.
