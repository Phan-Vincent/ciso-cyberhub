## Understand the concept

An attack surface is the set of exposed opportunities; a vector is a route used to reach a target. Email, removable media, exposed services, software updates and voice calls can all carry attacks. Reduce unnecessary exposure while improving identity verification and reporting.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

A request to change a vendor bank account may arrive through a genuinely compromised mailbox. A familiar address is not sufficient assurance. Verify the request through an established independent channel and a documented approval process. Avoid calling a phone number supplied only in the suspicious message.

## Practice and explain

List three entry points for a student project. For each, record the owner, intended users, exposed data and one practical reduction in exposure.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

How does an attack vector differ from an attack surface?

**Expected reasoning:** The vector is a route; the surface is the collection of reachable opportunities. An independent trusted channel can validate the request even if the mailbox is compromised.
