## Understand the concept

A baseline is a documented expected configuration with a process for deviations. Start from the system role, remove unnecessary services, apply supported updates and protect administrative access. Measure drift after deployment. Mobile and embedded systems may require different controls from general-purpose servers.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

On a Linux lab VM, record listening services and enabled startup units before modifying them. Disable only a service you understand and can restore. For wireless devices, assess authentication, encryption, management exposure and coverage; the strongest protocol setting cannot fix a shared administrative password.

## Practice and explain

Complete the Linux-hardening lab and capture one before/after control verification. Explain why the change did not break the intended service.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

What is a secure baseline?

**Expected reasoning:** An approved expected configuration that can be deployed, verified and maintained. A secure baseline requires continued monitoring and managed exceptions after deployment.
