## Understand the concept

Risk assessment considers uncertain events and their consequences. Record the asset, threat, weakness, impact, likelihood assumptions, controls and residual risk. Qualitative labels help compare items, but their meaning must be defined. Quantitative estimates are only as defensible as their input assumptions.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

For a lost-laptop scenario, estimate exposure using what data is present and whether keys were protected. Do not use the price of the device as the entire loss estimate. A risk register needs an owner and treatment decision: avoid, mitigate, transfer or accept. Transfer does not remove every responsibility.

## Practice and explain

Write two versions of a risk estimate using different likelihood assumptions. Explain which evidence would reduce the uncertainty and how the decision changes.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

What is residual risk?

**Expected reasoning:** Risk that remains after implemented controls and other treatments. Insurance can transfer certain financial consequences while operational risk remains.
