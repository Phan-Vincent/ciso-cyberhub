## Understand the concept

Identity management includes provisioning, changes, periodic review and timely removal. Apply least privilege to people and services. Separate normal activity from privileged administration and prefer short-lived credentials where feasible. MFA adds factors, but not every method resists phishing equally.

## Apply it to a realistic decision

When a student changes project teams, old access should be removed as new access is granted. A service account should have a named owner and a narrowly scoped role. Disabling the person’s account does not necessarily disable a separately created service credential.

## Practice and explain

Build an onboarding and offboarding checklist for a club repository. Include organization roles, tokens, deployment credentials, shared accounts and recovery access.

Write a short answer before checking the flashcard or quiz. Include the evidence you would collect, a likely failure case, and a limitation of your proposed control. Good security work makes assumptions visible and gives another person a way to verify the result.

## Check your understanding

Why separate privileged and everyday accounts?

**Expected reasoning:** It limits routine exposure of administrative authority and improves accountability. Personal offboarding must consider persistent service credentials and sessions as separate access paths.
