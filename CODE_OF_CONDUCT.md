# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect regardless of experience, identity or background. Critique evidence and technical decisions without personal attacks. Welcome questions, credit others' work and protect private information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, unauthorized access and sharing stolen credentials or exam dumps are unacceptable. Security research and competition activity must remain inside explicit authorization and event rules.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request corrections, restrict participation or refer serious incidents to the appropriate club or university process. They should document decisions, apply rules consistently and avoid conflicts of interest.

For public conduct concerns without private information, contact the repository maintainers. Before launch, the club must designate a private reporting channel and responsible reviewers; do not invent a contact address or post sensitive reports in a public issue. Emergency situations belong with appropriate local services or university support channels.
